  <h1>Reselling AI Privacy Policy</h1>
  <p><strong>Effective date:</strong> October 20, 2026</p>

  <p>Reselling AI is an inventory management application
  that helps organize resale products, marketplace
  listings, and sales information.</p>

  <h2>1. Information We Access</h2>
  <p>With account authorization, Reselling AI may access
  eBay account identifiers, active listings, product
  information, SKU numbers, order details, and
  transaction information.</p>

  <h2>2. How Information Is Used</h2>
  <p>This information supports inventory organization,
  listing matching, order review, and sales tracking.
  The current eBay integration uses read-only API
  requests and does not automatically publish listings.</p>

  <h2>3. Data Storage</h2>
  <p>Reselling AI primarily operates on the user's
  local computer. Account connection information and
  imported records may be stored in local files,
  which are not necessarily encrypted.</p>
  <p>Selected inventory information may be synchronized
  with Google Sheets. Cloudflare provides infrastructure
  for receiving eBay notifications.</p>

  <h2>4. Data Sharing</h2>
  <p>Reselling AI does not sell personal information.
  Information may be processed by configured services,
  including eBay, Google, and Cloudflare, as necessary
  for the application's functionality.</p>

  <h2>5. Data Retention and Deletion</h2>
  <p>Information is retained for inventory management,
  business operations, and applicable legal obligations.
  Privacy and deletion requests may be sent to the
  contact below. Applicable requests will be reviewed
  and handled in accordance with legal and platform
  requirements.</p>

  <h2>6. Account Authorization</h2>
  <p>Connecting an eBay account requires the account
  owner's authorization. Access may be revoked
  through eBay's account settings.</p>

  <h2>7. Changes</h2>
  <p>This policy may be updated as the application's
  functionality or data practices change.</p>

  <h2>8. Contact</h2>
  <p>For privacy questions or deletion requests,
  contact: njshavedice@gmail.com</p>
